How I work

People first

I try to be the kind of manager I'd want to have: honest, steady, and in the work with my team rather than above it. Most of my job is clearing the way so good people can do their best work and still have a life outside of it.

Humane AI

I'm hopeful about AI and use it every day. I also think it's on us to keep it safe and humane: protect people's private information, keep a person in the loop on decisions that affect someone's life, and be honest about what these tools get wrong.

Things I've built

Workshop

A calm home base for my workday as a manager: what to focus on next, and a clearer picture of what my team has on its plate. It runs entirely on my own computer, because work data shouldn't have to live on someone else's server.

Dispatch

Where I follow AI helpers as they take on coding tasks in small, reviewable steps. It runs on Tandem, my agent scaffolding, so nothing gets pushed, merged, or deployed without a person saying yes. I built it to see what helpful but supervised AI looks like in practice.

LifeKit

A growing collection of small tools for everyday work, from office helpers to classroom tools for teachers. Whenever someone around me has a task that could be easier, it usually ends up here.

TaskLedger In progress

A small phone app for capturing wins as they happen, so they're there when review season comes around. Links, emails, and ticket numbers are stripped out before anything reaches an AI model.
