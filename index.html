Engineering manager

Hi, I'm Alexis.

I'm an engineering manager who cares about the people on both ends of the software: the team building it and the people it affects.

I've spent my career in health care technology, where a bug isn't just a bug. It can be someone's coverage or a letter they never understood. That has shaped how I think about building things, and it matters even more now that AI is part of so much of the work.

Email me LinkedIn GitHub

How I work

People first

I try to be the kind of manager I'd want to have: honest, steady, and in the work with my team rather than above it. Most of my job is clearing the way so good people can do their best work and still have a life outside of it.

Humane AI

I'm hopeful about AI and use it every day. I also think it's on us to keep it safe and humane: protect people's private information, keep a person in the loop on decisions that affect someone's life, and be honest about what these tools get wrong.

What to work on next

A calm, keyboard-first task app. Give each task a value and an effort and it tells me what's next, then helps me plan the day around my sprint and wrap it up at the end.

  • Runs on its own
  • Android app
  • Connects to your tools
Open Docket docket.alexisdavidson.org

From request to resolved

Slate

Every request my team gets, on one board. A rough ask gets shaped into a clear brief, then I decide who builds it, and it moves across until it's merged and resolved. AI helpers do the legwork at each step, and nothing moves until I click.

  • One board, six columns
  • Sprints and team health
  • Connects to your tools
Open Slate

AI helpers, supervised

Dispatch

Where I follow AI helpers as they take on coding tasks in small, reviewable steps. I read their changes side by side and send feedback before any pull request exists, and nothing is pushed, merged or deployed without a person saying yes.

  • Runs on Tandem
  • One-line install
  • Your own AI provider
Open Dispatch dispatch.alexisdavidson.org

Receipts, 1:1s and goals

Rapport

Capture wins and feedback as they happen, for me and for each person on my team. It preps every 1:1 from what it already knows, keeps the team's goals as a tree, and turns it all into a review draft when the season comes. It grew out of TaskLedger, my old phone app.

  • Runs on its own
  • No AI needed
  • Nothing leaves your device
Open Rapport receipts.alexisdavidson.org
