Hi, I'm Alexis.

I'm an engineering manager who cares about the people on both ends of the software: the team building it and the people it affects.

I've spent my career in health care technology, where a bug isn't just a bug. It can be someone's coverage or a letter they never understood. That has shaped how I think about building things, and it matters even more now that AI is part of so much of the work.

How I work

I try to be the kind of manager I'd want to have: honest, steady, and in the work with my team rather than above it. Most of my job is clearing the way so good people can do their best work and still have a life outside of it.

I'm hopeful about AI and use it every day. I also think it's on us to keep it safe and humane: protect people's private information, keep a person in the loop on decisions that affect someone's life, and be honest about what these tools get wrong.

Things I've built

Workshop

A calm home base for my workday as a manager: what to focus on next, and a clearer picture of what my team has on its plate. It runs entirely on my own computer, because work data shouldn't have to live on someone else's server.

eng-agents

AI helpers that take on coding tasks in small, reviewable steps. They can't push, merge, or deploy anything without a person saying yes. I built it to see what helpful-but-supervised AI looks like in practice.

TaskLedgerprivate for now

A little phone app for noting small wins before I forget them. It scrubs out links, emails, and ticket numbers before anything is sent to an AI model.
